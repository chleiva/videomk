# Which music for which kind of video (2026-11-24)

**How this list was made.** The research found no reliable ranking of what people ask AI video tools to make: the sources are vendor blogs (marketing and product explainers first, educational explainers second, then tutorials, faceless channels and short social clips) and old YouTube category data (music, entertainment and gaming lead watch time). So the 20 types below are reasoned from those, from what the testers have actually asked (news, tech and AI, a city, places, marketing) and from the stock-music guides' genre advice (ambient for explanations, cinematic for high stakes, upbeat acoustic or pop for tutorials and motivation, quirky for comedy, lo-fi and chiptune for coding and games, broadcast beds for news). Treat it as a starting map to improve with real data.

Rule used for every row: **instrumental, steady, mid-tempo for explanations (about 90 to 115 bpm), higher energy only where the topic is itself energetic, and the mood of the video decides, not its keywords** (a news story about a disaster takes a solemn track, not a bright newsroom one).

## How the director uses it
It does not read this table. It reads each track's description in the catalogue (which names the kinds of topic it suits) and a "how to choose" rule in the prompt: decide the dominant feeling first, then match, match energy to the pace of the script, and vary the pick between videos. This table is the review sheet: after listening, remove a track from the library and the alternatives take its place; if a row has no track left that you like, regenerate one for that row.
