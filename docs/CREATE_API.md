# Creating a video: request check, options and capabilities (contract, decided 2026-11-25)

Owner decisions: a 15-45 s video still costs 1 credit (`MIN_MINUTES = 1`); vertical, square and 4:5 formats are being made to work now; **safety checks stop the video** (nothing is charged) and repeated violations are tracked per account; the planner may add about $0.03 and 25 s to a 3 minute video if the audit shows a quality gain; the owner reviews Spanish videos. Background and reasoning: `docs/PIPELINE_V3.md`, UI: `docs/UI_DESIGN.md` section (o).

## Creation options (all optional; absent means today's behaviour)
Sent in the body of `POST /users/{user}/videos/generate` next to `prompt`, `target_minutes`, `theme`, `captions`, `media`, `library_media`, `media_meta`, `max_images_per_minute`:
| Field | Values | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `platform` | `youtube`, `shorts`, `tiktok`, `reels`, `instagram_feed`, `linkedin`, `x`, `other` | `other` | What the video is for. Only informs recommendations and the director's pacing; the format fields decide the picture. |
| `aspect` | `16:9`, `9:16`, `1:1`, `4:5` | `16:9` | Frame shape. |
| `resolution` | `720p`, `1080p`, `2160p` (4K, only when capabilities lists it) | `1080p` | The short side in pixels is 720, 1080 or 2160: 16:9 is 1280x720, 1920x1080 or 3840x2160; 9:16 is 720x1280, 1080x1920 or 2160x3840; 1:1 is 720x720, 1080x1080 or 2160x2160; 4:5 is 720x900, 1080x1350 or 2160x2700. **`2160p` costs double the credits** (see 4K below). |
| `captions` | boolean | true | Burned-in subtitles. |
| `language` | `auto`, or a language id from capabilities (`en`, `es`) | `auto` | Narration language; `auto` = the language of the description (today's detection). |
| `brief` | object (see below), at most 2 KB | none | The answers to the clarifying questions, passed to the planner/director. |
| `watermark` | object `{mode, position, opacity, size, style, end_card, presence}` (see `docs/WATERMARK.md`) | the saved default, else the policy | **Free accounts (since 2026-11-27, QA and prod): every video carries the videomk lockup (mark + the word) for its whole length plus the closing "Made with videomk" card; whatever is sent is ignored and nothing is refused** (`capabilities.watermark.forced = true`, `forced_watermark` = the exact object). Premium accounts: `none`, their own logo (`custom`), or the videomk mark with `presence: "always"` or `"moments"` (fades in for about 4 s near the start and about every 30 s; their default). Listed in capabilities as `watermark` (modes, `style_options`, `presences` with `available`, positions, sizes, opacity range, `forced`). Do not send it unless the person changed it for this video. |
An unknown value is a 400 with a plain message. An `aspect` not listed as `ready` in capabilities is refused with 400 `{"message": "This format is not available yet"}`; a `resolution` that capabilities does not list is refused with 400 `{"message": "This resolution is not available yet"}` (a value that is not a resolution at all, e.g. `"4k"`, is 400 `'resolution' must be one of: 720p, 1080p, 2160p`). Nothing is charged on a 400. Credits depend on the length and on the resolution only: the started minutes (at least 1) times the resolution's `credits_multiplier` (1 for 720p and 1080p, 2 for 2160p); the shape and the platform never change the price.

## Capabilities
`GET /users/{user}/capabilities` (authenticated) -> what the apps may offer; the apps must work with only what is listed.
```json
{"aspects": [{"id": "16:9", "label": "Landscape", "ready": true, "beta": false}, {"id": "9:16", "label": "Vertical", "ready": true, "beta": true}],
 "resolutions": [{"id": "720p", "label": "720p", "credits_multiplier": 1, "aspects": ["16:9", "9:16"]},
                 {"id": "1080p", "label": "1080p", "credits_multiplier": 1, "aspects": ["16:9", "9:16"]},
                 {"id": "2160p", "label": "4K", "credits_multiplier": 2, "aspects": ["16:9", "9:16"], "note": "4K (2160p) costs twice the credits. Best for YouTube and large screens."}],
 "platforms": [{"id": "youtube", "label": "YouTube", "aspect": "16:9", "resolution": "1080p", "recommended_minutes": 3, "max_minutes": null, "other_aspects": ["1:1", "4:5"], "note": "Landscape 16:9 · 1080p · about 3 min", "supports_4k": true, "note_4k": null},
               {"id": "linkedin", "label": "LinkedIn", "aspect": "4:5", "resolution": "1080p", "recommended_minutes": 1, "max_minutes": 10, "other_aspects": ["1:1", "16:9"], "note": "Portrait 4:5 · 1080p · about 1 min", "supports_4k": false, "note_4k": "LinkedIn publishes at 1080p or lower; 4K would cost twice the credits for no visible gain."}, ...],
 "languages": [{"id": "en", "label": "English"}, {"id": "es", "label": "Español"}],
 "lengths": {"min_minutes": 0.25, "max_minutes": 12, "presets": [0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12]}, "max_photos": 100, "intake": true, "looks": {...see "Looks" below...}}
```
**Lengths.** `target_minutes` is a number from 0.25 (15 s) to 12; `presets` are the chips to show (15 s, 30 s, 45 s, 1 min, 2 min ...). Credits are the started minutes, at least 1: 15, 30, 45 and 60 seconds cost 1 credit, 61 seconds costs 2. Under a minute the engine writes a short-form script (about 2 to 4 beats, hook in the first 2 seconds), see `docs/SHORT_VIDEOS.md`.

**Platform fields.** `aspect` and `resolution` are the recommendation; `recommended_minutes` (always one of the presets) is the length the app should preselect when the person picks the platform (the person may still choose any length: the chosen length always wins in the engine); `max_minutes` is the platform's soft limit (null = none), for a hint such as "TikTok videos can be longer, but 30 s works best"; `other_aspects` are acceptable alternatives to show as secondary choices; `note` is the one-line text under the chips ("Portrait 4:5 · 1080p · about 1 min"). Sources and doubts: `docs/CREATE_OPTIONS_FLOW.md`. Everything is server-driven.

`beta: true` means it works but not every scene has a native layout yet (a framed fallback is used for those scenes).

**Resolutions and 4K (decided 2026-11-25; design and measurements: `docs/FOUR_K.md`).**
* `resolutions` lists **only the resolutions the person may choose right now**; the app shows exactly these, in this order, with `label` as the chip text (`4K` for `2160p`). 4K is behind a server switch: while it is off the list is `720p`, `1080p` and a request for `2160p` is a 400. Never hard-code the list.
* `credits_multiplier` (integer, on every resolution) is what the video costs relative to the same video at 1080p. The price the app shows is `max(1, ceil(target_minutes)) * credits_multiplier`: 3 minutes is 3 credits at 720p or 1080p and **6 at 4K**; 15 to 60 seconds is 1 credit, **2 at 4K**; 12 minutes at 4K is 24. The server works out the same number (one function, `common/wallet.credits_for_minutes`) and charges it when `generate` is accepted.
* `aspects` (on every resolution) are the frame shapes that resolution can be made in right now (ids from `aspects`); today it is every `ready` aspect for every resolution. If the chosen aspect is not in the chosen resolution's `aspects`, the app must not offer that pair.
* `note` (optional, only on resolutions that need one) is a one-line text to show next to the resolution when it is selected.
* `platforms[].supports_4k` is true only for `youtube` and `other`. `platforms[].note_4k` is `null` there and a ready-to-show sentence everywhere else. **When the person selects `2160p` while the chosen platform has `supports_4k: false`, the app must show `note_4k` as a clear recommendation against 4K** (and should offer one tap back to 1080p). It is a recommendation, not a block: if the person insists, `generate` accepts 4K for any platform and charges double. The platform's own `resolution` (the default the app preselects) stays `1080p` for every platform, including YouTube: 4K is always the person's explicit choice, never a default.
* `POST .../videos/generate` with too few credits answers HTTP 402 `{"message": "This video needs 6 credits and you have 5.", "code": "insufficient_credits", "balance": 5, "needed": 6}`; `needed` already includes the doubling, and for a 4K request the body also carries `"resolution": "2160p", "credits_multiplier": 2` (those two keys are absent for 720p and 1080p).
* A failed or rejected 4K video refunds everything it was charged (the doubled amount). Edits and re-renders of a 4K video stay 4K, cost no credits (as for every video), and their **previews are the same small draft as for a 1080p video** (960x540 for 16:9); only the final render is 4K. `GET .../videos` and the video item report `resolution: "2160p"` for a finished 4K video.
* A 4K video takes about 2 to 3 times as long to render as the same video at 1080p (roughly 15 to 20 minutes for a 12-minute video) and its file is about 2.3 times as large (about 70 MB per minute, at most 180 MB per minute): the app should say that it takes longer and should not assume the file is small when downloading or sharing.
* The `brief` request accepts `"resolution": "2160p"` in `options` like any other value; it never changes the questions.

## Looks: scene style and moving backgrounds (2026-11-27; `lambdas/capabilities_api.looks`, `engine/vector_policy.py`, `engine/background.py`, tests `tests/test_looks.py`)
What a client can ask for and what the server decides by itself. Capabilities says what THIS stage has switched on; read it instead of assuming.

**In capabilities** (`GET /users/{user}/capabilities`, labels and notes in the `X-Locale` language):
```json
"looks": {
  "style": {"param": "style", "default": "auto", "options": [
    {"id": "auto",    "label": "Auto",        "note": "We choose: photos and footage, with cartoon scenes only where they suit the topic.", "available": true},
    {"id": "cartoon", "label": "Illustrated", "note": "Adds animated cartoon scenes. Good for children's topics and simple ideas.",        "available": true},
    {"id": "real",    "label": "Real",        "note": "Only photos and footage, never cartoon scenes.",                                    "available": true}]},
  "backgrounds": {"animated": true, "loops_3d": true, "chosen_by": "director", "note": "Moving backgrounds behind text scenes are chosen for each video. Videos made from your photos keep a still background.", "ask": [...see below...]}
}
```
Spanish labels: `Automático`, `Ilustradas`, `Reales`. An older server has no `looks` key: then offer no style choice.

**Scene style: the `style` field of `POST /users/{user}/videos/generate`** (string; also accepted by `brief` in `options`).
| Send | Meaning |
|---|---|
| nothing, or `"style": "auto"` | The director decides. Cartoon scenes appear only where the policy allows (children's topics, everyday or abstract ideas, light-hearted, wellness; never news, real people, places or products, history, finance, medical, how-to that needs real pictures, LinkedIn, the person's own photos), and sparingly: at most about 20 percent of the scenes (40 percent for children's topics). Most videos get none. |
| `"style": "cartoon"` | The person wants illustrated scenes: up to 60 percent of the scenes are cartoon scenes from our catalog, when the catalog has scenes that fit the topic. The words "make it a cartoon", "illustrated style", "dibujos animados", "estilo ilustrado" in the request text do the same. |
| `"style": "real"` | Photographs and footage only, never a cartoon scene. The words "real footage", "no cartoons", "imágenes reales", "sin dibujos" in the text do the same, and win over cartoon words. |
| `"style": "whiteboard"` | The whole video is drawn and written by a marker on a whiteboard (docs/WHITEBOARD.md). The words "as a whiteboard video", "whiteboard style", "draw it on a whiteboard", "hand-drawn", "estilo pizarra", "en pizarra blanca", "dibujado a mano" in the request text do the same. On `auto` the director may choose it for explainers of processes and concepts, never for news, real people and places, photo or deck videos. Capabilities lists it as a fourth option (`Whiteboard` / `Pizarra`, with `say`), `available` by the server switch `WHITEBOARD` (QA on, prod off). |
* The field, when sent, wins over the words of the request. Any other value is a 400.
* `available: false` on `cartoon` means the cartoon scenes are switched off on this stage (prod today): hide or disable that option. Sending `"style": "cartoon"` there is still accepted and simply produces a video without cartoon scenes.
* A cartoon scene is one beat of the video: a flat-colour illustrated landscape or setting with gentle motion (drifting clouds, swaying plants, swimming fish, bobbing boats, birds, smoke), full screen in every format (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5), with the captions on top. They are settings, not characters: there are no people or animals acting out the story yet. A short video (under about 50 seconds; under 30 for children's topics) gets none.
* What the video got is recorded in its plan (`plan.vector`: decision, reason, the scenes used); the list and item responses do not carry it.

**Moving backgrounds: no request field, but the person can ask in the request text.** For every video that is not made from the person's own photos the director picks one background for the whole video: a pre-rendered 3D loop (`loops_3d`) or a procedural animated one (`animated`), by the topic's mood; a video that is mostly charts, tables, maps or code, and every photo video, keeps the still background. The background is only visible behind text and diagram scenes: full-screen clips, photographs and cartoon scenes cover it, so a video made mostly of footage shows little of it.
* **Asking by words** (read by the server from `prompt`; full table in `docs/HERO_LOOPS_3D.md`): capabilities lists the phrases this stage understands as `looks.backgrounds.ask`, already in the `X-Locale` language:
```json
"ask": [{"id": "gas-brand-dark",  "label": "Purple 3D gas",        "say": "with a purple 3D background"},
        {"id": "gas-ember-dark",  "label": "Warm 3D embers",       "say": "with a warm 3D background"},
        {"id": "gas-aurora-dark", "label": "Green 3D aurora",      "say": "with a green 3D background"},
        {"id": "gas-brand-light", "label": "Light 3D gas",         "say": "with a light 3D background"},
        {"id": "animated",        "label": "Any moving background", "say": "with an animated background"},
        {"id": "none",            "label": "Still background",     "say": "no moving background"}]
```
  (Spanish `say`: "con un fondo 3D morado", "con un fondo 3D cálido", "con un fondo 3D verde", "con un fondo 3D claro", "con un fondo animado", "con fondo fijo".) The four loop entries are present only where `loops_3d` is true; the list is empty where both switches are off. An app that wants to offer a choice appends the `say` text to the person's prompt (for example as a suggestion chip); there is nothing else to send. A named look also sets the video's theme unless the request carries `theme`.
* A wish never applies to a video made from the person's photos.

**A look that cannot be given as asked is adapted, never refused (2026-11-27; `lambdas/common/looks.py`, tests `tests/test_intake_looks.py`).** A wish about the look or style of the video (the `style` option, or words such as "cartoon", "animated characters", "explained by a fox", "whiteboard", "dibujos animados", "con personajes") is **never** a reason for `not_supported` or `blocked`. When this stage does not have the look (talking characters today; cartoon scenes and the whiteboard look on production) or does not use it for this kind of video (cartoon scenes, the whiteboard look and presenter characters are not used for news and real events, nor for videos of the person's own photos), the video is made in the closest look and the answer says so in two **optional** fields, on `brief` (`ok` and `questions` answers) and on the 202 of `generate`:
```json
{"status": "ok", "brief": {},
 "note": "Made as a narrated explainer with animated backgrounds; talking characters are coming soon.",
 "look": {"style": "auto", "asked": ["cartoon", "characters"], "adapted": ["cartoon", "characters"], "presenter": false}}
```
* `note` (string, one short sentence): present only when something was adapted. Show it as received, as a line on the Making screen or a toast. It is in the app's language (`X-Locale`); without that header, in the language of the request. Spanish: "Lo hacemos como un video explicativo narrado con fondos animados; los personajes que hablan llegarán pronto."
* `look` (object): present when the person asked for a look, by the option or in words. `asked`: the wishes read, any of `real`, `whiteboard`, `cartoon`, `characters`. `style`: what `generate` resolves the scene style to on this stage (`auto`, `cartoon`, `real`, `whiteboard`). `adapted`: the wishes that could not be met as asked (empty when all were). `presenter`: true when a presenter character will be offered to the director (only where the stage has presenters).
* Both are absent when nothing was asked: the answers are then exactly what they were. **An app must treat both as optional and must never stop a creation because of them.** They are a prediction made before the video starts, from the same words and server switches the engine reads; the director still decides scene by scene.
* The request check reads the looks of the stage from the same server switches as capabilities (`VECTOR_SCENES`, `WHITEBOARD`, `BG_ANIMATED`, `BG_LOOPS`, `PRESENTERS`), so the two cannot disagree. If the small model still answers "unsupported" with a sentence about cartoons, animation, characters or artwork, code drops that answer and the video goes on.
* What still stops a request: safety (`blocked`), and what the product cannot do at all (`not_supported`: a language other than English or Spanish, editing an existing video, live or real footage of a real person, something that is not a narrated video). The `not_supported` sentence is the product speaking ("VideoMK can't ..."), never "I"; a sentence that says "I" is replaced by "VideoMK can't make this one as described."

**Not available yet:** presenter characters (five characters exist with a working rig; they are being wired into real videos behind the server switch `PRESENTERS`, see `docs/PRESENTERS.md` when it lands). Until then a request for characters gets the note above.

## Request check (intake)
`POST /users/{user}/videos/brief` (authenticated), called by the app when the person taps Create, BEFORE `generate`. Nothing is charged here.
Request: `{"prompt": "...", "options": {platform, aspect, resolution, captions, language, target_minutes}, "media_count": 0, "answers": [{"id": "audience", "option": "kids"} | {"id": "tone", "text": "warm"} | {"id": "focus", "skip": true}]}` (`answers` empty on the first call; on later calls it holds ALL answers so far).
Response (always HTTP 200 unless noted; the app treats any failure or a response slower than 8 s as `ok` with no brief):
* `{"status": "ok", "brief": {...}}` : proceed; send `brief` in `generate`. May carry the optional `note` and `look` (see "Looks": a look wish that was adapted; never a reason to stop).
* `{"status": "questions", "questions": [{"id": "audience", "text": "Who is this for?", "options": [{"id": "kids", "label": "Kids"}, ...], "allow_other": true}, ...]}` : at most 3 questions, each with 2 to 5 options, each answerable by one tap; the app shows them one at a time with Skip / 'You decide' and 'Make it now'. Questions are asked only when the answer would change the video materially and are never about things the form already captures (platform, format, length, captions, language, look). Clear requests get `ok` with no questions.
* `{"status": "not_supported", "message": "...", "suggestion": "..."}` : the product cannot do this at all (unsupported language, editing an existing video, live footage, not something a narrated video can be); kind and short, written as the product ("VideoMK can't ...", never "I"); nothing was charged. **Never returned for a look or style wish** (cartoon, animated characters, whiteboard ...): those videos are made, with a `note`.
* `{"status": "blocked", "message": "..."}` : safety stop. Neutral wording, no lecture, never names the policy detail; nothing was charged; recorded against the account.
* HTTP 429 `{"status": "paused", "message": "...", "retry_after": 3600}` : the account is temporarily paused after repeated safety stops (see Safety).
Question `id` is one of `audience`, `tone`, `focus` (which meaning, angle or period), `details` (a missing personal detail); an option's `id` is its label lower-cased with underscores (`complete_beginners`), so the answers carry all state. An option answer sets the field of its question id; free text on its own sets that field too; free text next to an option, or any text on `details`, goes to `notes`; `skip` adds nothing. Answers that are only option taps are merged without a model call. Invalid input (unknown id, bad option, text over 200 characters, more than 8 answers) is HTTP 400 `{message}`.
`brief` object (what `generate` accepts, validated server side by `common/intake.clean_brief`: an object of at most 2 KB, else 400; unknown keys dropped, `summary` recomputed; may be `{}` when nothing was asked): `{"audience": "kids", "tone": "calm", "focus": "causes", "notes": "free text from Other (<= 200 chars)", "summary": "For kids · Calm · Focus: causes"}`; every value is a short string.

## Language: the `X-Locale` header (decided 2026-11-26; full policy and glossary in `docs/I18N.md`)
* **Every call the apps make carries `X-Locale: en` or `X-Locale: es`**: the device language mapped to the two supported languages (any `es-*`, such as `es-MX` or `es-419`, is `es`; anything else is `en`). It is sent on every endpoint, authenticated or not, together with `X-Region` and `X-Timezone`. A `lang` query parameter is not needed and not read.
* The server picks the language from `X-Locale`; without it from the browser's `Accept-Language` (best supported language by q-value); else English. A request with no locale information gets the English text exactly as before. An unknown `X-Locale` (say `fr`) means English.
* What follows the locale: the `message` field of every error a person can cause or see (credits, limits, photos, library, account, editor, "not found"), the safety `blocked` and `paused` messages, and the **labels and notes in `GET .../capabilities`** (`aspects[].label`, `platforms[].label` of "Just a video", `platforms[].note`, `platforms[].note_4k`, `resolutions[].note`). What never changes with the locale: ids, `code` and `status` values, numbers, flags, language names (`English`, `Español`), and protocol errors for malformed requests (such as "'media' must be a non-empty list"), which the apps never show.
* The `not_supported` message and the intake questions and option labels are written by the model in the language of the request (the description), not of the app. The narration language is a separate choice (`language`: `auto`, `en`, `es`).
* Responses that depend on the locale (capabilities) carry `Content-Language: es|en` and `Vary: X-Locale, Accept-Language`, so a cache never serves Spanish labels to an English app. The apps should refetch capabilities when the app language changes.
* Safety messages: with `X-Locale` they are in the app's language; without it, in the language of the request text (the previous behaviour).
* Web: the browser sends `X-Locale` from the saved language choice (else `navigator.language`). CORS preflights allow the header on every route.
* Cognito hosted sign-in: pass `lang=es` on `/oauth2/authorize` for Spanish (the web does).

## Safety (owner decision: a safety stop stops the video)
* Both `brief` and `generate` run the safety check on the request (the app cannot skip it by calling `generate` directly); a `blocked` verdict means no video, no charge, HTTP 400 `{"status": "blocked", "message": "..."}` from `generate`.
* Every block is recorded per account with a category, a time and a short excerpt kept 90 days (for review and abuse tracking; disclosed in the privacy policy). Zero-tolerance categories put the account on hold at once; otherwise 3 blocks in 24 h pause creation for 24 h, 8 in 30 days hold the account for review. A hold or pause returns 429 `paused`; staff clear it with `scripts/safety_admin.py`. `generate` also returns 429 `{status, message, retry_after}` with a `Retry-After` header. Details, thresholds and procedures: `docs/INTAKE_AND_SAFETY.md`. Log lines `SAFETY_STRIKE` and `SAFETY_HOLD` carry the account id and category only.
* If the model check cannot run, a deterministic screen of obvious terms decides; the director's own `legit` check remains a backstop.

## Making a video from a presentation deck (PowerPoint or PDF; flag `deckToVideo`, contract decided 2026-11-26)
Full design, measurements and security model: `docs/DECK_TO_VIDEO.md`. The feature exists only where the server says so: `GET .../capabilities` carries a `deck` object **only when it is on**; hide the whole feature otherwise.
```
"deck": {"max_mb": 50, "max_slides": 60, "formats": ["pptx", "pdf"]}
```
The flow has three calls before `generate`, so the person sees problems and the credit price **before** paying.

1. **`POST /users/{user}/decks`** `{"format": "pptx"|"pdf", "size": <bytes>}` -> 200 `{"deck": {"id", "format", "url", "fields"}, "max_bytes", "expires_in": 900, "max_mb", "max_slides", "formats"}`. Upload the file with a multipart form POST to `url` (the returned `fields` first, then `file` last), like photos. The slot fixes the key, the content type (`application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.presentationml.presentation` for pptx, `application/pdf` for pdf) and a size range of 1 byte to 50 MB; send exactly that `Content-Type` in the form fields. Errors: 400 `deck_unsupported` (format not pptx/pdf, bad size), 400 `deck_too_large`, 429 `too_many_decks` (10 per day), 404 `unavailable` (flag off). `.pptm`, `.ppt`, `.key`, `.odp` are not accepted: the app should offer "Export as .pptx or PDF" for those (a Google Slides or Keynote export is a PDF or PPTX).
2. **`POST /users/{user}/decks/{id}/process`** (after the upload finished) -> 202 `{"status": "processing"}`; repeating it is harmless. 400 `missing` if the upload did not finish, 404 `missing` for an unknown deck (including another person's: the id only exists under its owner).
3. **`GET /users/{user}/decks/{id}`** every 2 seconds (processing takes about 5 to 30 seconds) -> `{"id", "status": "uploading"|"processing"|"ready"|"failed", "format", ...}`.
   * `ready`: `"slides"` (n), `"suggested_minutes"` (the length the deck gets if the person does not choose one: about 20 seconds per slide, between 0.5 and 12 minutes) and `"suggested_credits"` (what that costs at the default resolution; the usual rule: started minutes, 4K doubles it). Show "12 slides, about 4 minutes, 4 credits" and let the person change the length with the usual length control.
   * `failed`: `"code"` and a localized `"message"` to show as is. Codes: `deck_too_large`, `deck_too_many_slides`, `deck_unsupported`, `deck_corrupt`, `deck_encrypted` (password protected or old `.ppt`), `deck_macro` (macros, embedded programs or scripts), `deck_empty`, `deck_unsafe`, `deck_convert_timeout`, `deck_convert_failed`. All say what to do next ("Export it as PDF", "Save without a password"). A deck still `processing` after 10 minutes is reported `failed` with `deck_convert_timeout`.
4. **`POST /users/{user}/videos/generate`** with `"deck": "<id>"` (the id of a `ready` deck). `prompt` becomes **optional** (it steers audience and tone: "for executives", "for students"; a short default is used when absent). `target_minutes` optional (absent: the deck's `suggested_minutes`, charged exactly that); when present it wins and the narration is condensed or expanded to fit. `language` (default `auto`: the deck's own language; Spanish decks get Spanish narration), `aspect`, `resolution`, `platform`, `captions`, `theme`, `brief` work as for any video. **A deck and photos are mutually exclusive** (`deck` with `media` or `library_media` is a plain 400 with a localized `message`, like other invalid options): they are different stories (the photo path curates a story from pictures; a deck already has its own), and mixing them would need a second storyboard pass for no demonstrated need. Errors: 404 `unavailable`, 400 `deck_missing` (not found, not yours, already used or expired after 2 days), 409 `deck_not_ready` (call `process`/poll first), 400 with any of the `failed` codes above, plus the usual 402 `insufficient_credits` and 400/429 safety answers. A deck is used by one video: the files are deleted when planning starts.
5. **Progress** in the video item (`GET .../videos`): `status` is `PLOT_CREATION` while the deck is read and the story written, and the item also carries `"stage"`: `"deck_reading"` (preparing the slides), then `"deck_writing"` (understanding the slides and writing the narration). Show "Reading your slides..." and "Writing the story..." while `status == PLOT_CREATION`; afterwards the usual states (`ASSETS_CREATION`, rendering, `COMPLETED`) apply and `stage` should be ignored. `stage` is absent for videos that are not made from a deck. A deck the safety check stops, or that cannot be used, ends as a rejected video (`reject_reason`, localized, credits refunded), exactly like a rejected prompt.
6. **What the apps must send**: `X-Locale` on every call above (messages and codes follow the person's language; the `code` never changes), `X-Timezone` on `generate` as always. Never log or cache deck contents; the app never needs to read the file after uploading it.

What the video is: the person's slides stay on screen exactly as designed (rendered to pictures; **animations, transitions and embedded video/audio are ignored, every slide shows its final state**; bullets do not appear one by one), a narrator explains each slide, the camera pushes in on the part being discussed, and slides cross-fade. Fonts the server does not have are substituted (Calibri, Cambria, Arial, Times, Courier are metric-compatible), so line breaks can differ slightly. Set this expectation in the upload sheet: "Your slides appear as they are. Animations are not included."

## Retry a video, remove a video that did not finish (2026-11-27; `lambdas/video_retry.py`, tests `tests/test_video_retry.py`)

"Try again" on a video that did not finish reuses **that video**: same id, same list item, status back to making. A client must **never** build a new `generate` request for a retry (that is how a video lost its 60 photos): it calls the route below, and the server runs the stored request again with every photo, option, language and watermark.

* **`POST /users/{user}/videos/{video_id}/retry`** (Cognito, no body; send `X-Locale`).
  * `202 {"message": "workflow restarted", "video_id", "status": "STARTED_PROCESSING", "retries": n, "photos": n, "photos_unusable"?: n}`: the same item is being made again.
  * `200 {"message": "already running", "video_id", "status", "retries"}`: a second tap; nothing started, nothing charged. Treat like 202.
  * `409 {"code": "attachments_unavailable", "message", "photos", "missing"}`: some photos can no longer be restored. Nothing started, nothing charged. Show the message; the person creates the video again with their photos.
  * `409 {"code": "retry_limit"}` (3 retries per video), `409 {"code": "rejected"}` (a safety decision is never retried), `409 {"code": "not_failed"}`, `409 {"code": "no_request" | "deck_used"}`.
  * `402 {"code": "insufficient_credits", "balance", "needed"}`: the failed attempt was refunded; the retry is charged like a new video.
  * `404 {"code": "not_found"}`: not the caller's video (ids cannot be probed). A 404 **without** that code, or 403/405, means an older server without the route: show "not available", do not resend.
  * `429` with `Retry-After`: the account is paused.
* **Cancel a video that is being made: `POST /users/{user}/videos/{video_id}/cancel`** (Cognito, no body; send `X-Locale`). New 2026-11-27 (`lambdas/video_retry.py cancel_handler`, tests `tests/test_video_cancel_delete.py`).
  * `200 {"message": "cancelled", "video_id", "status": "CANCELLED", "retries": n, "refunded": <credits given back, e.g. 2>}`: the workflow was stopped, the item is `CANCELLED`, the credits of this attempt are back in the wallet (into the buckets they came from), the working files are cleared. The photos the video was made with are kept, so "Try again" works.
  * `200 {"message": "already cancelled", "video_id", "status": "CANCELLED", "retries": n, "refunded": 0}`: a second tap or a retry of the call. **Idempotent: credits are refunded exactly once per attempt** (one conditional write decides who cancels; the refund itself is once-only on the item). Treat like the first 200.
  * `409 {"code": "not_cancellable", "message", "video_id", "status"}`: the video is not being made (`COMPLETE`, `FAILED` or `REJECTED`; also when it finished while the request was on its way: then nothing is refunded and the finished video is there). Refresh the item.
  * `409 {"code": "try_again", "message", "video_id", "status"}`: the video moved from one making step to the next between our read and our write (rare). Nothing changed. Send the same call once more.
  * `404 {"code": "not_found"}`: not the caller's video (ids cannot be probed). `403`: the `{user}` in the path is not the caller. `401`: no token. `400`: malformed id. A 404 **without** that code, or 403/405 from API Gateway, means an older server without the route.
  * Which statuses can be cancelled: every status that is not `COMPLETE`, `FAILED`, `REJECTED` or `CANCELLED` (today `STARTED_PROCESSING`, `PLOT_CREATION`, `ASSETS_CREATION`). An edit that is re-rendering a finished video cannot be cancelled here.
  * **Afterwards** `GET .../videos` and `GET .../videos/{id}` show the item with `"status": "CANCELLED"`, no `making` object, and `"can_retry": true|false` like a failed one. A cancelled item can be tried again (`POST .../retry`, charged like a new video) or removed (`DELETE`). A step that was already running when the workflow was stopped can no longer change the item's status.
* **Delete a video: `DELETE /users/{user}/videos/{video_id}`** (Cognito, no body; send `X-Locale`). Since 2026-11-27 this also deletes a **finished** video (before: only one that did not finish).
  * `200 {"message": "deleted", "video_id"}`: the item is gone from the list, and so are its files: the finished MP4s of every revision, previews and GIF (`videos/<user>/<video>/`) and its working files including its own copies of the photos (`generation/v2/<video>/`). Signed URLs already handed out stop working. **Never touched:** the person's My media library (another bucket), their pending uploads, their watermark logo, any other video.
  * Deletable statuses: `COMPLETE`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, `REJECTED`. Credits: deleting a finished video refunds nothing (it was delivered); a failed, cancelled or rejected one was already refunded (and is refunded here, once, if that never happened).
  * `409 {"code": "not_deletable", "message", "video_id", "status"}`: the video is being made. Cancel it first (`POST .../cancel`), then delete.
  * `409 {"code": "busy", "message", "video_id", "status"}`: an edit of this finished video started rendering less than 20 minutes ago. Try again when it is done.
  * `404 {"code": "not_found"}`: not the caller's video, **or already deleted**: a repeated `DELETE` answers 404 `not_found`, which the app treats as success (remove the card). `403`, `401`, `400` as for cancel.
  * There is no undo and no bin.
* **List** (`GET .../videos`): a `FAILED` or `CANCELLED` item carries `"can_retry": true|false` (false after 3 retries or for a presentation video, whose deck was used up). Offer "Try again" only when it is true; always offer "Remove".
* **Wallet: when the next free credit arrives. `GET /users/{user}/wallet`** (Cognito; send `X-Timezone: <IANA name>`, e.g. `Europe/Madrid`) answers `{"balance", "unlimited", "buckets": [{"kind", "remaining", "expires_at"}], "next_daily_at", "next_free_credit_at", "next_free_credits", "rules": {...}}`. New 2026-11-27: **`next_free_credit_at`** (ISO 8601, UTC, e.g. `"2026-11-28T23:00:00Z"`) and **`next_free_credits`** (how many arrive then, today 1). `next_daily_at` (the same moment as epoch seconds) was already there and stays.
  * The moment is worked out exactly as the daily credit is granted (`common/wallet.py ensure_grants`): the person's **local day** (the timezone in `X-Timezone`; UTC when absent or unknown) and the UTC day must both have moved on since the last claim, so it is the later of "the next local midnight" and "the next UTC midnight after the last claim". For almost everyone that is simply the next local midnight. Format it in the device's timezone ("Next free credit: tomorrow", or the hour when it is today).
  * The credit is not pushed: it is added the first time the wallet is read or a video is started at or after that moment. So at that time call `GET .../wallet` again; the new balance includes it and `next_free_credit_at` moves a day on.
  * A new account's first free credit comes on the day after sign-up (the welcome credits cover the first day). The daily credit expires at the end of its local day (it is in `buckets` with its `expires_at`).
  * When daily credits are switched off on the server: `next_free_credit_at: null`, `next_daily_at: null`, `next_free_credits: 0`. Hide the line.
  * The `402 insufficient_credits` answer of `generate` and `retry` is unchanged (`balance`, `needed`); read the wallet for the date.
* **Clients**: iOS, Android and web (`frontend/src/components/app/useVideos.ts`, `lib/videos.ts retryOutcome`) update the card in place (the item becomes the making card), then poll as usual.

## Making status (2026-11-27; `lambdas/common/making.py`, `lambdas/engine/making_progress.py`, tests `tests/test_making_status.py`)

What the Making screen shows (`docs/DESIGN_BAR_2026.md` section 3 item 3): finished scenes fill a film strip, one plain line says what is happening now, the sentence being narrated sits beneath. The apps get it from the calls they already poll; nothing new to call.

* **Where:** every item of **`GET /users/{user}/videos`** that is being made gains one optional object, `making`. The same object is also in the 409 answer of **`GET /users/{user}/videos/{video_id}`** (`{"message", "status", "making"?}`), for a screen that follows one video. Additive: older clients ignore it, and no other field changed.
```json
"making": {
  "scene_total": 9,
  "scene_done": 4,
  "live_line": "Adding footage of sunlit canopies",
  "narration_line": "Sunlight pours through the canopy, and the forest breathes.",
  "scenes": [{"index": 1, "done": true, "thumb_url": "https://…"}, {"index": 2, "done": true}, {"index": 3, "done": false}]
}
```
* **When it is there.** Absent until the plan exists (`STARTED_PROCESSING`, `PLOT_CREATION`: show your own "writing" state), present from then until the video is `COMPLETE`, absent for `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, `REJECTED` and finished videos. Absent for a video that was already in flight when this shipped. **Every field is optional**: draw what is there.
* **`scene_total`**: the planned scenes. **`scene_done`**: the scenes whose voice and picture are stored. It only counts what is really known and **never goes down during a run** (it is counted from a set that only grows). Scenes are made up to 12 at a time, so they do not finish in story order: use `scenes[].done` for the strip, `scene_done` for "Scene 5 of 9" (announce `scene_done + 1`, capped at `scene_total`). "Try again" starts a new run: `making` disappears until the new plan exists, then counts from 0 again.
* **`scenes`**: one entry per planned scene in story order, `index` from 1. `thumb_url` is there only for a done scene that has a picture: a stock photograph or one frame of a stock clip, the person's own photo, the first photo of a gallery or collage, a slide. A scene the engine draws (title, text, chart, diagram, map) has no picture before the render, so it has **no `thumb_url`**: show the plain filled tile. The picture is a JPEG 320 px wide (its own shape kept, never enlarged; crop it to the tile).
* **`thumb_url` is private and short-lived**: signed for 10 minutes, only ever returned to the owner of the video by these two authenticated calls, a new URL on every poll. **Cache the picture by video id and scene `index`, not by URL** (a scene's picture does not change during a run), and never store or share the URL. The files are deleted with the video's working files (7 days at most).
* **`live_line`**: one short plain sentence (at most 8 words, no step names, no numbers) in the app's language, from **`X-Locale`** (`en`, `es`; anything else is English). Show it as received. It describes the scene being worked on (the first scene not done yet): "Adding footage of sunlit canopies", "Finding pictures of bearded vulture in flight", "Placing your photos", "Placing your slides", "Narrating “Why GPS needs relativity”", "Recording the narration"; then "Putting the scenes together" once every scene is done (the render), and "Adding the finishing touches" during the last step. The subject is named only when it is written in the app's language: stock searches are worded in English and titles in the video's language, so a Spanish app mostly gets the plain sentence ("Añadiendo video a esta escena", "Buscando la imagen adecuada", "Grabando la narración") and names only Spanish titles ("Narrando «La ruta de mis patatas»").
* **`narration_line`**: the first sentence of the narration of the scene being worked on, in the **video's** language (not translated, at most 180 characters). Once every scene is done there is no single scene in work, and it is the video's opening sentence. Absent for a scene without narration (a pause).
* **What it does not say.** The render and the final stitch have no per-scene progress: during them `scene_done == scene_total` and only `live_line` changes. There is no percentage and no time estimate here; keep the app's own estimate.
* **Server side.** The plan step stores a compact list of the planned scenes on the video record (`making_plan`); each finished beat adds itself to `making_done` (and to `making_thumbs` when its small picture was stored at `generation/v2/<video>/thumbs/<beat>.jpg`); the last step sets `making_step`; all four are removed when the video is complete. No model call and no extra render: per scene one small read, one resize (or one frame grab from the clip) and one small write, inside the step that is already running. The two status functions may read `generation/v2/*/thumbs/*` and nothing else of the working files.

## Plans: limits, locked options, the free plan's state, the gallery (2026-11-27; full contract in `docs/PLANS.md`)

Where the server has plans on (QA and production from the next deploy), `generate`, `retry`, capabilities and the wallet follow the account's plan (Free, Pro, Max). Nothing can be bought yet: **`can_upgrade` is `false` everywhere and no app may show a buy button**; show the plan names and "coming soon".

* **Send `X-Platform: ios` or `X-Platform: android` on every call** (next to `X-Locale`, `X-Timezone`, `X-Region`). The web sends nothing. Free credits are granted and spent only from the mobile apps.
* **`GET .../capabilities`** gains `plan` (`id`, `name`, `state`, `period_end`, `renews`, `managed_by`, `limits`, `locked`, `plans`, `plan_credits`), `free_creation` (`none` | `welcome` | `daily`), `free` (`available`, `reason`, `message`, `credits_left`, `max_seconds`, `budget_paused`, `resumes_at`) and `can_upgrade`. The lists keep **every** option and say which are locked: `resolutions[].available` / `resolutions[].requires_plan` (put "Max" on the 4K tile), `lengths.plan_max_seconds` and `lengths.locked` (`[{over_seconds, max_seconds, requires_plan}]`: badge the lengths over the plan's longest), `plan_max_photos`, `watermark.modes[].requires_plan`. Nothing is granted by this call.
* **`GET .../wallet`** gains the same `plan`, `free_creation`, `free`, `can_upgrade`; `buckets` may hold kind `plan` (the monthly plan credits, with their `expires_at`). With the default settings `next_free_credit_at` is `null` (the daily credit is off): hide that line.
* **`GET /users/{user}/plan`**: the plan screen (`plan`, `plans`, `free`, `purchases`).
* **Free plan today (defaults, all server settings):** 2 welcome credits once (valid 30 days) from the mobile app, no daily credit, videos up to **30 seconds**, up to 1080p, always the videomk watermark with the name and the end card. If `free.available` is `false`, do not show an empty or broken Create: show the gallery and the plans, with `free.message`. `free.reason`: `off` (gallery-only mode), `app_only` (web), `not_available`, `paused` (today's free videos are used up for everyone; back at `resumes_at`), `used`, `monthly_cap`.
* **New answers of `generate` and `retry`** (nothing was charged; `message` is localized; all carry `plan`, `requires_plan` and `can_upgrade`):

| HTTP | `code` | Meaning | Show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 403 | `plan_length` | longer than the plan's longest video (`limit.max_seconds`) | the upgrade prompt for `requires_plan` |
| 403 | `plan_resolution` | e.g. 4K below Max | the same |
| 403 | `plan_photos` | more photos than the plan uses (`limit.photos_per_video`) | the same |
| 403 | `plan_watermark` | a watermark mode the plan lacks (`none` on Free) | the same |
| 403 | `watermark_premium` | own logo below Max (now with `requires_plan: "max"`) | the same |
| 403 | `free_not_available` | free credits cannot be used on this request; `reason`: `app_only` or `not_available` | `message`, the gallery and the plans |
| 429 | `free_budget_spent` | today's free videos are used up for everyone; `resumes_at` | `message` ("back tomorrow") and the plans; the person's credits are untouched |
| 429 | `free_monthly_cap` | this account's free videos for the month are used | `message` and the plans |
| 402 | `insufficient_credits` | unchanged, now also `plan` and `can_upgrade` | as before |

  A request **without** `target_minutes` is never refused for length: it gets the server default shortened to the plan's longest (30 s on Free). A request without `resolution` gets the plan's best when 1080p is above it.
* **Watermark**: on Free the request may still choose `position` and `size`; the mode, the name and the end card are fixed. `PUT .../watermark` with `mode: "none"` on Free answers 403 `watermark_required` with `requires_plan: "pro"`.
* **`GET /gallery`** (public, no token): the showcase of videos made by videomk, for signed-out screens and for Free accounts that cannot create. `{"videos": [{id, title, title_i18n, description, description_i18n, prompt | null, aspect, shape, width, height, seconds, lang, video_url, poster_url | null}], "count", "expires_in": 3600}`. The links last an hour: fetch the list again rather than storing them. An empty list is a valid answer.
* **Device proof** (mobile, Free accounts only, where the server has the routes): `POST /users/{user}/device/challenge`, then `POST /users/{user}/device/verify` with an App Attest attestation or assertion (iOS) or a Play Integrity token (Android). Flow and fields: `docs/PLANS.md` section 7. Needed in production before a Free account can create; QA works without it.
* **Purchases** (`POST /users/{user}/purchases/apple|google`): the routes exist and answer **501 `purchases_unavailable`** until store purchases are built; request and response shapes are in `docs/PLANS.md` section 9.
