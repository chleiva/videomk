videomk

Describe it. We’ll make the video.

Say or type what you want to understand, add a few photos if you like, and videomk makes a narrated explainer video, with pictures, clips, music and subtitles.

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon.

How it works

  1. 1

    Describe it

    Type or say your idea in a sentence. Pick how long it should be.

  2. 2

    We make it

    Script, voice, pictures and music come together in a few minutes. You can leave while it works.

  3. 3

    Share it

    Watch it, edit any scene, then share or save the video.
