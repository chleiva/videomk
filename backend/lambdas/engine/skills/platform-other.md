- The video may be watched anywhere, often embedded on a page, with or without sound: it must make sense muted, with captions on, and stand on its own with no platform context.
- Open on the payoff or the question in the first 5 seconds; no logo-first intro, no "welcome".
- Length: use the length asked for; 1-3 minutes is the sweet spot for one idea (viewers watch most of it); longer videos need chapters and a re-hook about every 2 minutes.
- Structure: promise, 2 to 5 clear steps or points with an example each, one takeaway, one next step.
- Pace: a new picture every beat (10 to 14 seconds of narration at most); vary scene types; diagrams, charts and timelines are fine in landscape.
- On-screen text: key terms, numbers and labels only, normal landscape amounts; captions on, away from the bottom 8%.
- Tone: clear, friendly, neutral and professional; plain words.
- Music: quiet under the voice; no sound effects.
- Ending: one takeaway and a single concrete next step that is true to the request. No invented links or brand.
- Avoid: slow intro, dependence on audio, walls of text, filler.
