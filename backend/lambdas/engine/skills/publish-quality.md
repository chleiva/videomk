name: Publish quality
always: true
---
- Accuracy first. Every number, date, name and claim comes from the grounding notes. If something is uncertain or disputed, say so and name the source ("according to ..."); if you cannot support it, leave it out.
- The title is specific and promises the payoff the video delivers (at most 60 characters, no bait you cannot pay off). The description is two plain sentences that use the topic's own words.
- Chapter titles say what the viewer learns ("Why the river floods"), not "Part 2".
- Assume some viewers watch without sound: the key term, number or name of each beat is on screen. Define jargon at first use. Never rely on colour alone to carry meaning.
- Cut filler: no "in this video", no "as we have seen", no repeating a point to fill time. Use the whole requested length with substance.
- The last beat leaves one clear takeaway or next step, in the viewer's terms.
- Respect: nothing that defames a private person, and no instruction presented as medical, legal or financial advice. On contested topics show the main views fairly and say what is known and what is not.
