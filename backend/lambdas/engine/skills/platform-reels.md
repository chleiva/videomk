- Visual first, sound off friendly: the first frame is a striking picture or one big word that already tells the topic; the spoken hook follows within 2 seconds. No greeting, no logo.
- Length: 15 to 30 seconds for a punchy single idea, up to 45 to 60 seconds for a story or a three-step explanation that earns it. Completion and replays are the signals to earn, so shorter is safer.
- Structure: hook, 2 to 4 beats of payoff, a clean last line. A loop (last line returning to the first) is encouraged; Reels favour replays and shares.
- Pace: beats of 8 to 16 words (a new picture every 3 to 6 seconds), a clip montage on visual topics; polished, clean images over busy diagrams; clips and strong photographs shine here.
- On-screen text: 2 to 6 big words per beat, in the middle of the frame. Keep text and captions out of the top 14% and the bottom 35% of the frame height (the caption and buttons cover it on Reels) and out of the right 12% of the width. Captions on, short lines.
- Tone: warm, confident, a little aspirational; plain words; no clutter.
- Music: soft bed under the voice, low energy; voice clear.
- Ending: the last line is a short, memorable takeaway or a loop back to the opening, never a trailing summary; a "save this" or "send it to someone who..." line only if natural for the topic (saves and shares are rewarded signals).
- Avoid: low-resolution or watermarked images, text in the bottom third, slow intro, dense charts, reposted-looking content, more than three items.
