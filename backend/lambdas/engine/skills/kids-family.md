- Short sentences, everyday words, one idea at a time. Explain through things a child knows: animals, food, toys, playgrounds.
- Open with a question or something surprising, and ask the viewer a question now and then ("Can you guess?").
- Repeat the key idea in a new way near the end; repetition is how children learn.
- Be warm, playful and safe: nothing frightening or violent, no unsafe instructions, no brands or personal data.
- Keep facts accurate and from the notes; simple is not the same as wrong.
- Use counting, comparison and colour; keep the pace gentle with room to look.
- Visuals: large simple pictures, a pictogram for counting, a definition card for one new word. Music: playful and gentle.
