pick: Motivational, inspiring, success stories, self-improvement, mindset, habits, overcoming obstacles, speeches.

- Build on one specific story or person, with real details, not generic encouragement. Specifics move people; slogans do not.
- Shape: where they started, what they wanted, the obstacle, the turning point, what changed, what it means for the viewer.
- Show the cost and the struggle honestly; effort and setbacks make the win believable. Avoid "anyone can" promises and survivorship claims you cannot support.
- Speak to one person with "you". Plain, warm, short sentences; let a few lines breathe.
- Offer one small action the viewer can take today, not ten.
- Do not invent quotes, achievements or numbers; use only those in the grounding notes.
- A quiet beat before the key line and again before the close lets it land.
- Visuals: a quote for the line to remember, a timeline for the journey, strong photographs of real places and moments. Music: hopeful and slowly building, never bombastic.
