- The feed plays muted and silently while people scroll: the message must be understood from pictures and big text alone in the first 3 seconds; the spoken hook backs it up. Open on the strongest picture with 2 to 5 big words.
- Length: 15 to 30 seconds is the safe default; one idea; up to 60 seconds only for a clear step-by-step. Under 20 words per beat.
- Structure: hook picture and line, 2 to 4 beats of payoff, one closing line. A loop-friendly end works well.
- Pace: a new picture every 3 to 5 seconds; simple scenes (image, stat, quote, icons, bullets up to 3 short items); calm but lively.
- On-screen text: large and short (at most 6 words), key term of the beat, centred, with a margin of about 8% of the width on the sides. In the 4:5 frame keep text out of the bottom 10% and top 8% (the feed crops 4:5 on the profile grid, so keep the key picture and text in the centre 3:4 area).
- Captions on, burned in, 2 lines at most.
- Tone: friendly, visual, brand-safe, plain words; show the idea, do not lecture.
- Music: gentle under the voice.
- Ending: one takeaway; "save" or "share" invitation only if natural. No invented handles or links.
- Avoid: text-heavy slides, dependence on audio, a slow intro, tiny text, key content near the edges.
