name: Biography and profile
pick: The life of a person, 'who was X', profiles, founders, artists, scientists, leaders, 'the story of <person>'.
---
- Choose the question that defines the life ("How did a clerk change physics?") and answer it. A biography is an argument about one life, not a list of dates.
- Shape: the world they were born into, what drove them, the obstacle, the turning point, the work or deed, the cost, the legacy.
- Show the person through specific scenes and choices, with exact years and places from the notes.
- Be fair: success and failure, credit and controversy, in proportion and with sources.
- Use only quotes and facts that appear in the grounding notes; never invent a thought or conversation.
- End with why they still matter and one thing the viewer will notice differently.
- Visuals: a profile card with photo and years, a timeline for the life, photographs of places and works. Music: thoughtful, warm, emotional but restrained.
