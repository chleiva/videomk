- Native, not polished: the first line is spoken within 2 seconds and speaks straight to the viewer ("Nobody tells you this about...", "You've been doing X wrong"). No intro, no logo, no title card. The first frame already shows motion or the key picture.
- Length: 20 to 40 seconds by default; up to about 60 seconds only when the story keeps adding new information. One idea per video.
- Structure: hook, tension or surprise within 5 seconds, quick proof or steps, payoff, last line that rewards finishing or invites a replay (a loop back to the hook is ideal).
- Pace: fastest of all platforms: beats of 8 to 14 words (a new picture every 3 to 5 seconds), a clip montage where the topic is visual, no pause beats, no static chart or diagram; prefer image, clip, stat, kinetic text, quote.
- On-screen text: 2 to 5 words per beat, large, centred in the middle band. Keep all text and captions out of the top 12% and the bottom 25% of the frame height and out of the right 14% of the width (the caption, username and buttons sit there). Captions burned in, 4 to 6 words a line.
- Tone: casual, conversational, a little personality; short sentences; contractions; no corporate or lecture voice, no hype you cannot back.
- Music: a calm bed under the voice (trending sounds are not available); the voice always clear.
- Ending: the last line is a short, quotable payoff or one concrete thing to do now ("So just start building today."); stop right after it. A question that invites a comment only if it fits. No "follow for part 2" unless the request has one.
- Avoid: slow build-up, a long preamble, text-heavy slides, more than 3 list items, corporate tone.
