name: Product and marketing
pick: Promoting a product, app, service, company or idea; 'marketing video for', launches, pitches, ads, landing-page videos.
---
- One audience, one problem, one promise. Name the viewer's problem in their own words in the first beat, make it feel real, then show the way out.
- Structure: problem, what it costs the viewer, the solution in one sentence, how it works in three simple steps, proof, one clear next step.
- Benefits before features: say what changes for the viewer, then the feature that makes it so. Use their words, not internal jargon.
- Proof must be real: a number, a result or a named customer from the notes; never invent a testimonial, statistic or award.
- No superlatives you cannot prove ("best", "revolutionary"). Specific beats loud.
- Keep it short and brisk; one idea per beat; end with a single call to action stated plainly.
- Visuals: a counter or stat for the headline number, steps for how it works, versus for the before and after, icons for the benefits. Music: upbeat and confident, steady.
