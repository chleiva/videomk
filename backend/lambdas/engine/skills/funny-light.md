name: Funny and light-hearted
pick: Humour, fun facts, myths and mistakes, weird history, 'funny', parodies, light and playful explainers.
---
- Comedy is specificity: the odd real detail beats a general joke. Use true, surprising facts from the notes and let the facts be the humour.
- Understate. State the absurd thing plainly and calmly, then stop. Do not explain the joke or announce that something is funny.
- Rule of three: two normal items, the third a surprise. Put the punch word last in the sentence.
- Use contrast and callbacks: bring an earlier absurd detail back near the end.
- Timing: a short beat before a punchline and after it lets it land; keep the lines short.
- Keep it kind: punch at situations, ideas and institutions, never at vulnerable groups or private people.
- Keep it accurate: invented "facts" are not jokes. Mark exaggeration as exaggeration.
- Visuals: a counter or scale comparison for absurd numbers, a myth-vs-fact card, a surprising photograph. Music: light, quirky, bouncy.
