---
id: explainer-education
name: Educational explainer
pick: How something works, what something is, why something happens; concepts, ideas, 'explain X', school and university topics.
---
- Open on a concrete puzzle or a common misconception ("Why does ice float?"), not a definition. The viewer should want the answer.
- One big idea for the whole video. Every beat serves it; cut the interesting detours.
- Build from the familiar to the new: an everyday comparison first, then the precise term, then the exact version. Name a term only after the idea it labels.
- One idea per beat. After two or three beats, say in one line where we are and what comes next.
- Give one worked example with real numbers or a real case, and walk it step by step.
- Correct the misconception explicitly before or while teaching the right idea.
- End by restating the one idea in a single plain sentence and what the viewer can now do or notice.
- Visuals: steps or a flow for processes, a chart only for real numbers, a definition card for a key term, a timeline for sequences.
