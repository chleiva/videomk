name: Mystery and unsolved
pick: Mysteries, unsolved cases, disappearances, conspiracies explained, strange events, true-crime style stories.
---
- Open on the unanswered question and one chilling, factual detail. Reveal in the order the evidence became known, not all at once.
- Use only facts from the grounding notes and name their source. Separate proven facts, reasonable inference and speculation, and say which is which.
- Never accuse a real private person. Describe what was alleged or established in court, and say when someone was cleared or never charged.
- Be respectful to victims and families: no gratuitous detail, no sensational language.
- Keep tension honest: hold back the answer, never a fact the viewer needs. Close each section with the next open question.
- End with what is still unknown or what the evidence supports best.
- Visuals: a timeline for the order of events, a map for locations, a headline card for quoted reports, photographs of places rather than people. Music: quiet, creeping, restrained.
