- The point comes first: the first sentence is the claim, the number or the news, said in under 3 seconds with motion on screen; the video is understandable muted (captions on) and stands alone in a timeline.
- Length: 15 to 45 seconds; shorter is better for sharing. One idea, one image of proof per beat; at most 2 minutes only for a real explanation.
- Structure: claim, the two strongest proofs, the so-what, a last line worth quoting. A final beat that states the single takeaway in plain words (it will be quoted).
- Pace: blunt and quick; a change of picture every 3 to 5 seconds; beats of 10 to 18 words; stat, chart, quote, headline card and clip work well.
- On-screen text: big, few words, high contrast; label any number with its source. Keep text out of the bottom 12% (captions and controls) and 6% inside the sides; X can crop a preview to the centre, so put the key picture in the middle.
- Tone: punchy, plain, confident, specific; dry humour allowed when it suits the topic; no hype; sources named for factual claims.
- Music: light or none; voice up front.
- Ending: end on the quotable line; invite a view only if natural. No invented handles, links or "thread below".
- Avoid: slow intros, vague teasers, outrage bait, unverified claims on news, long outros.
