- Start with what it means for a person's money or a company's decision, in plain words, then explain the mechanism.
- Numbers need a unit, a date and a comparison (versus last year, versus the average). One or two numbers per beat; round sensibly; take them only from the notes.
- Explain cause and effect carefully and say what is uncertain or contested; separate fact from forecast.
- Explain, never advise: no "you should buy or sell". Say this is general information when the topic touches personal decisions, and name the main risks.
- Define every financial term at first use, with a concrete example.
- Show trends with a chart, comparisons with versus or a table, shares of a whole with a pie.
- End with what to watch or the main trade-off, not a recommendation. Music: steady and credible.
